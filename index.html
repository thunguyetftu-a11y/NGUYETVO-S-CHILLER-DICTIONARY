📚 Learning Rules & Scoring

⏱️ How It Works

  • Each question has 10 seconds to answer
  • Questions are randomly shuffled each time you play
  • Answer before time runs out to score points
  • After 10 seconds, automatically moves to next question
  • No turning back to previous questions

⭐ Scoring System

Answered in 1 sec: 10 + 5 = 15 points
Answered in 2 sec: 10 + 4 = 14 points
Answered in 3 sec: 10 + 3 = 13 points
Answered in 4 sec: 10 + 2 = 12 points
Answered in 5 sec: 10 + 1 = 11 points
Answered in 6-10 sec: 10 + 0 = 10 points
Wrong answer: 0 points

🎯 Points Breakdown

  • Standard Points (Blue): 10 points for each correct answer
  • Bonus Points (Orange): Extra points for answering quickly (within 5 seconds)
  • Maximum per question: 15 points (10 standard + 5 bonus)

🏆 Leaderboard

  • Your total score appears in the leaderboard after each quiz
  • Top 10 scores are saved locally on your device
  • Compete with friends by sharing the program link
